Why Join ASME-Tech?

We're committed to supporting all students interested engineering technology by providing them with a community and opportunities to expand their skills. We're open to all majors, simply join the discord and pay dues to join.

We host a variety of events, including:

  • Day builds: Give you the opportunity to learn new skills through short beginner friendly projects.
  • Socials: Get the chance to meet more engineers.
  • Workshops: Learn skills to help advance your academic and professional careers.
